Field manual

Instructions

  • Tap a pipe to rotate it a quarter-turn. Right-click (or long-press) rotates it back. Ctrl+Z undoes your last turn.
  • Water runs from the red gate valve — the spoked handwheel is your shutoff. It cranks open when the water releases. A pipe only fills when both ends of the joint line up — just like a real fitting.
  • Fixtures are drawn the way they read on a set of plans — toilet,sink,tub,shower,dishwasher. When the water reaches a fixture, its symbol fills blue — the plan comes alive.
  • An open end with water behind it is a leak: it drops your pressure and fails the inspection. Every run on these jobs ends at a fixture — you don't just cap a line off and call it done.
  • You pass when every fixture is live and pressure holds at 100%.
  • Race the water: on your first turn the water is released — but it holds a few seconds before it starts creeping from the valve, so you always get a breath to read the board and make your opening move. Then it fills one run at a time. If the front reaches an open end, the job floods — keep the run ahead of the water leak-free, watch the Water front gauge, or switch the race off for a relaxed rough-in.
  • Live lines are set (race on): once the water front has reached a pipe, that pipe is set — turning a fitting on a live line lets the joint go and floods the job. Do your turning ahead of the water, and leave what's wet alone. With the race off there are no live lines at all: turn any fitting freely, for as long as you like.
  • Don't leave the valve disconnected: if the entry fitting leaves the line cut off at the valve while the race runs, a countdown starts on the valve itself. Reconnect the line before it runs out or the pressure blows the valve and floods the job. Reconnecting resets the countdown. The valve gives you 5 seconds early on, one more per district, up to 10 in the Master district.
  • The pressure test can bite: finishing the route starts a pressure test while the lines fill — and on later jobs, a pipe on your finished run can burst mid-test. Rotate it back into line before the pressure drains. A fast save keeps your stars; a slow one costs one. The first couple of jobs never pull this on you.
  • Rusted joints: some pipes deal rusted shut — a rust-brown fitting will not turn on the first tap. That tap breaks the rust loose and counts as a move; the next tap turns it. The Book counts the break on any rusted pipe that needs turning.
  • The last guy's work: some Career jobs start with a section already plumbed in by the last guy — his fittings wear a brass lock and will not turn, ever. Sometimes he got it right and the section is a head start; sometimes he got it wrong and his pipe sits in your way like a piece of furniture. You can't move it, so you route around it — every one of those boards is still beatable, and the Book already counts his section as placed.
  • Reading pauses the water: opening these Instructions or the Patch Notes during a run pauses the water front and the valve clock until you close them — read at your own pace.
  • Stars & ranks: stars are earned with the race on — pass with the race off and the job still counts as done, but no stars are banked for it. Fast, clean jobs earn up to three stars, and every passed job is graded on the trade ladder — Helper, Apprentice, Journeyman, Master. Journeyman takes every job through The Practical (Job 201) passed with at least two stars on each one; Master takes the whole board — all 300 jobs at three stars. Your career rank rides on the hub and the Job Board, and your stars are saved on this device.
  • Coffee breaks: the Hint button trades a short break for a hint — one wrong pipe flashes gold. If a job floods, one rescue per attempt pumps the water back and gives you one more chance without losing the run.
  • Modes: the Daily Job is a new house every day, the same for everyone playing that date — and it deals hard now. One official attempt per day: the score posts only on a first-attempt win. Restart it, flood it, or close the page mid-run (it resumes right where you left it), and the day plays on as practice — stars and the Book stamp still count, the score just stays home. The Job Board shows the campaign jobs you have passed plus the next one waiting, across six districts — Maple, Riverside, Hillcrest, Old Town, Lakeside, and the Master District — and every single one of them is beatable; the rest of the map stays hidden until you reach it. Endless Depths keeps dropping deeper houses past the campaign.
  • Sound: with sound on, a soft underscore runs under the race — barely there while you're safe, surfacing and quickening only when the water nears an open end.
  • Districts & stamps: each district has its own colour, its own say on the job brief, and its own mix of fixtures. Clear all 50 of a district's jobs at three stars and the district earns its stamp on the Job Board — a landmark on the climb between ranks.
  • The foreman: the first time a rule bites — a live line, the valve clock, a rusted joint, stars and the race — the foreman says the rule out loud, once per rule. After that, you're expected to know it.
  • Run replays: every run is recorded as you play it. Watch any finished run back from its win or flood card, watch a job's best run from the Job Board, and save a replay as a file you can drop into Discord. Only your most recent run and one replay per job's best run are kept. Jobs beaten before replays arrived have none saved until you beat them again.

The Toolbox — earned perks

Tools are something you get for levels. Pass campaign jobs and every 10 on the books restocks every tool you carry — beat the Book on three or more jobs in a block and that restock pays premium, double charges. Tools spend in Career and Endless only: the Daily runs clean, no tools on the truck. No tool is ever required to solve a board — they're rescue and flair, never the only way through.

  • Fixture Finder Job 10 — Pulses every fixture still waiting for water.
  • Deep Undo Job 20 — One tap takes back your last three turns.
  • Chalk Line Job 25 — Career replays only: spend a charge to snap the finished line from your best run on this job onto the board in chalk for five seconds — the water keeps running while you look. Jobs beaten before this update have no saved line until you beat them again.
  • Free Spin Job 30 — Arm it, and your next pipe turn doesn't count toward the total.
  • Deep Hint Job 40 — A senior hand turns one dry, wrong pipe right for you. Counts as a turn.
  • Snake Cam Job 50 — The camera snake lights the whole true route for three seconds.
  • Patch Kit Job 60 — Mid pressure test, seals a burst pipe on the spot — no scramble.
  • Slow the Flow Job 80 — The water front crawls at half pace for 15 seconds.
  • Pipe Freeze Job 100 — Freeze a plug of ice in the line — the front stops dead for 10 seconds.
  • Second Rescue Job 150 — One more rescue in a run that's already used its rescue — spend it from the flood card.
  • Valve Shut-Off Job 200 — Arm it, then turn one live line safely — the valve's shut, this once.
  • Push Back the Water Job 200 — The front rolls back a stretch and hands you the section back.
  • Drain the Whole Line Job 300 — Every drop in the pipes drains out — the front restarts at the valve.
  • Golden Valve 25 Book-beats — Pure shine: your valve renders gold. No charge, no cost.
